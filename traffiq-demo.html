Webster Optimum Cycle Length Equation

Delay Minimizer
$$C_0 = \frac{1.5L + 5}{1 - Y}$$
where:
• $L$ = Total lost time per cycle ($2 \times 4.5s = 9.0s$)
• $Y = \sum y_i$ = Summation of critical approach flow ratios ($y_{NS} + y_{EW}$)
• $g_i = (C_0 - L) \times \frac{y_i}{Y}$ = Proportional green interval
